Refund & Return Policy

Last Updated: June 15, 2026

Thank you for shopping at Netrima Jewels. We take immense pride in our craftsmanship and want you to be completely delighted with your purchase. In the event that you are not satisfied with your selection, we offer a comprehensive return and refund policy outlined below.

1. 30-Day Return Window

We provide a 30-day money-back guarantee for all standard products purchased online. You can return eligible items within 30 days from the delivery date for a full refund or exchange, subject to inspection. Custom designs or customized engravings are not eligible for returns.

2. Eligibility Criteria for Returns

To qualify for a refund, the returned jewellery must satisfy all of the following conditions:

  • The item must be in its original, pristine condition, showing no signs of wear, scratches, damage, resizing, or alteration.
  • All original packaging must be returned, including the luxury inner gift box, outer protection box, and padding.
  • You must provide the original sales invoice, along with all associated certificates of authenticity (BIS Hallmark, IGI, GIA, or brand certificates). Returns cannot be accepted without these certificates.
  • Security tags or labels attached to the piece must remain completely intact.

3. Non-Returnable Items

Certain products cannot be returned or refunded:

  • Custom-made designs, bespoke orders, and products with customized engravings requested by the customer.
  • Gold coins, gold bars, or silver coins.
  • Items returned past the 30-day return window.
  • Products that show signs of modification or damage not reported immediately upon delivery.

4. Return Pickup and Shipping

We provide free, fully insured return shipping within India for eligible returns:

  • To initiate a return, contact our customer support team via email or WhatsApp. We will coordinate a pickup date.
  • Our authorized logistics partner will pick up the package. Do not hand over the package without verifying the pickup agent's identity.
  • Ensure the package is sealed securely using the packaging materials provided during collection to maintain insurance coverage.

5. Inspection and Processing

Upon receiving your package, our state-of-the-art laboratory and expert gemologists will inspect the piece:

  • We verify that the returned product matches the original description, serial numbers, metal weight, gold purity, and diamond clarity/weight.
  • The inspection process generally takes 2 to 4 business days from receipt of the item at our fulfillment facility.
  • If the item passes inspection, we will approve the refund. If the item shows signs of wear, resizing, or missing certificates, the return may be rejected or subject to a restocking fee.

6. Refund Method & Timelines

Once approved, refunds are processed immediately:

  • Refunds are credited back to the original payment channel used during purchase (credit card, debit card, net banking, or UPI via Razorpay).
  • For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, we will request your bank details to process the refund via NEFT / IMPS transfer.
  • Refunds normally take 5 to 7 business days to reflect in your account, depending on your financial institution.
